  {\LARGE \textbf{Ninth International Olympiad, 1967}}\\
\end{center}

\textbf{1967/1}\\
Let \(ABCD\) be a parallelogram with side lengths \(AB = a,\;AD = 1\), and with \(\angle BAD = \alpha\).  If \(\triangle ABD\) is acute, prove that the four circles of radius \(1\) with centers \(A,B,C,D\) cover the parallelogram if and only if
\[
a \le \cos\alpha + \sqrt{3}\,\sin\alpha.
\]

\textbf{1967/2}\\
Prove that if one and only one edge of a tetrahedron is greater than \(1\), then its volume is \(\le \tfrac18\).

\textbf{1967/3}\\
Let \(k,m,n\) be natural numbers such that \(m + k + 1\) is a prime greater than \(n + 1\).  Let \(c_s = s(s+1)\).  Prove that the product
\[
(c_{m+1} - c_k)\,(c_{m+2} - c_k)\cdots(c_{m+n} - c_k)
\]
is divisible by the product \(c_1 c_2 \cdots c_n\).

\textbf{1967/4}\\
Let \(A_0B_0C_0\) and \(A_1B_1C_1\) be any two acute-angled triangles.  Consider all triangles \(ABC\) that are similar to \(\triangle A_1B_1C_1\) (so that vertices \(A_1,B_1,C_1\) correspond to \(A,B,C\), respectively) and circumscribed about \(\triangle A_0B_0C_0\) (where \(A_0\) lies on \(BC\), \(B_0\) on \(CA\), and \(C_0\) on \(AB\)).  Of all such possible triangles, determine the one with maximum area, and construct it.

\textbf{1967/5}\\
Consider the sequence \(\{c_n\}\), where
\[
\begin{aligned}
c_1 &= a_1 + a_2 + \cdots + a_8,\\
c_2 &= a_1^2 + a_2^2 + \cdots + a_8^2,\\
&\;\;\vdots\\
c_n &= a_1^n + a_2^n + \cdots + a_8^n,
\end{aligned}
\]
in which \(a_1,a_2,\dots,a_8\) are real numbers not all equal to zero.  Suppose that an infinite number of terms of the sequence \(\{c_n\}\) are equal to zero.  Find all natural numbers \(n\) for which \(c_n=0\).
